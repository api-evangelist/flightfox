# Flightfox

> Flightfox is a managed travel platform for complex group travel, headquartered in Boulder, Colorado. It helps educational institutions, humanitarian and faith-based organizations, and corporate teams book and manage flights, hotels, and ground travel through self-serve booking software backed by 24/7 human travel-agent support. Flightfox does not currently publish a public developer API, SDKs, or developer documentation.
